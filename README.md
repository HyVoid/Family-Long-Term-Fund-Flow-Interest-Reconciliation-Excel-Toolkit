### What this tool helps you do

* Reconstruct long-term two-way fund movements into a consistent historical record.
* See how much has been advanced, repaid, and remains outstanding as principal.
* Calculate accrued interest across changing time periods using configured market rates.
* Reconcile historical transactions even when they were originally entered out of chronological order.
* See the current outstanding amount split between principal and accrued interest.
* Update new transactions or interest assumptions while keeping the same calculation workflow.

### Why this tool exists

Long-term family financial arrangements often become difficult to reconcile simply because the transactions accumulate over many years. Bank records, notes, transfers, repayments, and changing interest assumptions can leave you with a large historical trail but no simple answer to the question: **What is the balance today?**

You could repeatedly ask AI to build another spreadsheet, or manually reconstruct the calculation each time. The problem is that the thinking has to be repeated.

This workbook packages that specific reconciliation workflow into a reusable Excel tool. Instead of starting with a blank workbook every time, you can keep one structured record of the historical transactions, update the assumptions when needed, and return to the same calculation framework.

### What makes this toolkit different

**Built Around Historical Reconstruction**

The workbook is designed specifically for situations where transactions have accumulated over a long period. Historical entries are reordered chronologically before the running principal and interest calculations are performed, helping keep the calculation sequence consistent.

**Separate Transactions From Calculation Assumptions**

Transaction records and calculation assumptions are kept separate. You can maintain the transaction history while independently defining the currency symbol, day-count basis, interest mode, transaction direction, and historical market-rate periods.

**A Clear Answer Instead of a Long Transaction List**

The calculation is ultimately reduced to the figures that matter: total outflows, total repayments, net outstanding principal, accrued interest, and current total outstanding. This makes the workbook useful for reviewing the overall financial position rather than simply storing historical transactions.

### Quick Start
